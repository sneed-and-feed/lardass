01

Three-Eyed Characters

Surrealist figurative character heads defined by third-eye iconography, concentric circular contours, exaggerated facial pores, and drooping lower-jaw drips. Often accessorized with bowler hats or jester crowns.

02

Dot-Field Textures

A distinct replacement for conventional spray fades, applying high-contrast secondary color dot rings (orange across emerald, lavender across orchid) along letterforms and borders.

03

Freight Steel & Industrial Cuts

Focus centered on transcontinental grain hoppers, autorack sills, and heavy concrete infrastructure across bridge abutments and riparian rip-rap zones in the Lower Mainland.
